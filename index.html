About Us

Recogni was created to support families caring for loved ones living with early-stage dementia. We built Recogni to make everyday care easier, safer, and more connected while respecting each person’s independence.

Recogni brings together voice-first assistance, gentle medicine and routine reminders, safe-zone alerts, location updates, and simple family communication. Our goal is to provide helpful support without making technology complicated or overwhelming.

Our mission

Making everyday care easier, safer, and more connected.
